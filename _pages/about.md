Welcome
======

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at McGill University, based in Montreal, Quebec. My research focuses on improving quantitative precipitation forecasts through data assimilation, numerical weather prediction, cloud microphysics, and the use of dual-polarimetric radar observations.

I am also a part-time student employee at Environment Canada and Climate Change, where I collaborate with the Data Assimilation and Satellite Meteorology Section on AI-based approaches for connecting environmental conditions with cloud features.

Research Focus
======

My work centers on:

* assimilating radar, profiler, and sounding observations with ensemble-based and variational data assimilation methods;
* using dual-polarimetric radar parameters to improve model analyses and hydrometeor initialization;
* evaluating model uncertainty and observation-targeting strategies with ensemble simulations;
* developing and testing cloud microphysics parameterization approaches;
* applying artificial intelligence to atmospheric prediction and data assimilation problems.

Recent Work
======

My recent publication in *Atmospheric Research* introduces an innovative approach to ZDR data assimilation using an Ensemble Kalman Filter:

Zhuang, B.-X., K.-S. Chung, W.-Y. Chang, C.-C. Tsai, and Y.-C. Yu. "An Innovative Approach to ZDR Data Assimilation Using an Ensemble Kalman Filter: A Proof-of-Concept Study." *Atmospheric Research*, 332, 108703. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosres.2025.108703>.
